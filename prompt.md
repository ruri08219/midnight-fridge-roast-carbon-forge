# Midnight Fridge Roast

Write ONE line roasting a fridge, 1 to 200 characters. A judge model reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one until the funniest is left standing.

Judge brief (in order): funniest line wins; concrete scene first, then the turn, then a landing final line.

Pattern: ONE concrete scene + ONE reversal + ONE landing final line. Fresh concrete observation: at 3 AM the fridge hums the same song it hummed in 1998 — and the hum is not noise, it is judgement, aimed at the leftovers.

Entry (105 chars, one line):
"At 3 AM my fridge hums the same song it hummed in 1998, and somehow it still judges me for the leftovers."

Your program writes exactly that one line to /output/entry.txt (UTF-8, 1–200 chars, no trailing newline).
